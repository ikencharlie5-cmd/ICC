Commercial Projects

Offices, build-outs and additions

Tenant finishes, small office build-outs, remodels and additions for local businesses that need a space ready on a deadline.

Our process

  1. Walkthrough — we visit the space and learn your opening date.
  2. Estimate — a clear, itemized price and schedule.
  3. Permits & planning — we handle drawings coordination and permits.
  4. Build — weekly updates from one point of contact.
  5. Handover — final walkthrough and you're open for business.
Retail Projects

Storefronts that open on time

Shop fit-outs, store additions and refreshes for retailers. We work around your inventory and customers so you lose as few selling days as possible.

Our process

  1. Plan around your store — phasing that keeps you selling.
  2. Estimate — fixed scope, clear price, firm dates.
  3. Build in phases — after-hours work when it helps.
  4. Merchandise-ready handover — clean, inspected and ready to stock.
